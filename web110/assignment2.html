The next topic I am describing is installing multiple web browsers to compare the different programs. After using Chrome for most of my life, it is interesting to see how the other browsers handle the UI. Each browser has its own strengths and weaknesses. Chrome, for example, is really fast and compatible with a lot of extensions, and it also automatically syncs everything with your Google account for even faster access and usability. Firefox is nice for being open-source and highly customizable to users' preferences; the only problem is it’s not as well optimised as Chrome, so it falls short. Safari is nice and works well with Apple security and privacy features, and is convenient since it already comes on iPhone, but it will always have the stigma of being “Safari”. At the end of the day, all these browsers do the same thing, and it's ultimately up to the user what they prefer.
