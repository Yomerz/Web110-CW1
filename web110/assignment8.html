The next topic I am describing is research on password managers and multi-factor authentication. These are very useful in cybersecurity. As the internet evolves, so do hackers, making it increasingly important to protect your online passwords from strangers who want them for malicious purposes. It's not only important to have strong passwords, but also to store them somewhere secure, whether on a locked hard drive or in a password manager. Password managers, however, have one flaw: if they get broken into, the system you are using should encrypt its data well so it's hard to decrypt. It is a great add-on in cybersecurity; we even use it at Harper.
